    Movement & Transitions

    A large part of my work centred on rotations and transitions, keeping motion smooth across a huge range of situations. Any jitter or hitch was mine to track down and sand away, with quaternions used throughout to avoid gimbal lock.

    Gameplay footage courtesy of GameSpot.

    Physics & Engine Foundations

    I contributed broadly to the physics systems and nativised Blueprint logic into C++ for performance. I also helped expand core Unreal Engine fundamentals for the team, such as primary data assets for designers, and used ImGui extensively for in-game debugging.

    Input & Data-Driven Tuning

    I handled some input and wired it through into the physics systems. Most of the behaviour is exposed to designers as data rather than hardcoded in C++, and I built debug tooling to visualise and tune it live, which made tracking down unwanted results far faster than guesswork.

    Gameplay footage courtesy of GameSpot.
